### How are the prices calcuated?

That is a great question. There are actually three main components

- The primary driving factor of the prices is actual (real) log data from warcraftlogs.com.
- Additionally, demand for stocks also influence prices, but not as much as logs. Buy -> number goes up. Sell -> number goes down.
- Finally, a small intermittent wobble randomly moves the prices up or down to make things more interesting.

#### That makes sense, how do logs affect prices?

- The majority (~60%) comes from personal growth i.e. is warrior improving from last week? The rest (~40%) compares you to the other tank or dps warriors in each raid.

- Abilities also have their own weights depending on if they are tanks or DPS. For example, Bloodthirst has a bigger effect on prices than Whirlwind.

- Everything is normalized against kills/wipes, etc. So killing fewer enemies one week won't make it appear warriors are doing less overall and hurt their value.

- Prices are compounded each raid (more raids = more price), but there is a grace period for new warriors so their first raids do not hurt them too much (that's the healers's jobs).
